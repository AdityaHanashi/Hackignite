Guidelines & Safety

Rules of Conduct and Safety

Please read all event guidelines and safety policies carefully before participating.

01
Harassment, discrimination, or inappropriate behaviour towards any participant, mentor, volunteer, or staff member is strictly prohibited.
02
Abusive language, threats, or aggressive conduct are not allowed.
03
Damage to venue property or equipment is not allowed.
04
Plagiarism, use of pre-built projects, or any form of cheating is not allowed.
05
Alcohol, tobacco, or any prohibited substances are not allowed on the premises.
06
Any team found guilty of misbehaviour or violating these rules will be disqualified immediately, with no exceptions. The decision of the organising committee is final.
07
These rules apply equally to coordinating members, organisers, and volunteers. Any coordinator found misbehaving or misusing their position will face strict action, including removal from the event team.
08
Participants are not permitted to leave the venue during the hackathon. In case of an emergency, inform a volunteer or the help desk immediately.
09
If you face any issue or feel unsafe at any time, contact the help desk at [location] or speak to any volunteer. All complaints will be handled promptly and with confidentiality.
10
Keep your belongings secure and carry your ID card at all times.
